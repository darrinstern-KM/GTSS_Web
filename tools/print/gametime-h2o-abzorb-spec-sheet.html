Features

  • Gets games back on. Absorbs standing water and mud on contact to shorten rain delays and prevent rainouts.
  • Works everywhere on the field. Infield skin, mound, home plate area, warning track and wet turf.
  • Fine, fast gradation. More surface area per pound means faster drying than a standard conditioner.
  • No waste. Once the field is dry, drag it into the infield mix, where it keeps managing moisture.
  • Will not stick to cleats. Calcined clay holds its shape instead of turning to mud.
Recommended for: Baseball, softball, football, soccer, parks and recreation, youth leagues.
Application: Spread by hand or shovel over wet spots, let it absorb, then rake or drag it in. Use sparingly on slick areas during play.

Contains naturally occurring crystalline silica. Avoid creating and breathing dust during application. A safety data sheet is available on request. Values shown are typical and may vary slightly between lots.

California Proposition 65 warning: this product can expose you to crystalline silica, which is known to the State of California to cause cancer. For more information go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.
