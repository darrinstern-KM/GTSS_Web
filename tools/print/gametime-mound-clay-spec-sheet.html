Features

  • Built for the high-wear areas. The front of the rubber, the landing area, and the batter's and catcher's boxes.
  • Easy to work. Moldable right out of the bag, with no mixing.
  • Packs tight. High clay content compacts into a firm surface that resists digging out.

  • Construct, rebuild or repair. Use it alone for daily maintenance or with GameTime Clay Bricks for a full rebuild.
Recommended for: Baseball, softball, parks and recreation, youth leagues.
Application: Add in 1 inch layers, lightly water, and tamp each layer firm and level.

Contains naturally occurring crystalline silica. Avoid creating and breathing dust during application. A safety data sheet is available on request. Values shown are typical and may vary slightly between lots.
