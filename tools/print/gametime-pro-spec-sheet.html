Features

  • Manages moisture. Absorbs water when the field is wet and releases it as the skin dries, so the surface stays playable longer.
  • Reduces compaction. Hard, stable particles keep permanent pore space in the infield mix.
  • Improves drainage. Water moves through the skin instead of pooling on it.
  • Holds up. Resists crushing underfoot, so it does not break down into dust or mud.
  • Looks the part. A clean, consistent red finish across the whole infield.
Recommended for: Baseball, softball, parks and recreation, youth leagues.
Application: Topdress the skin and drag it in, or till it into the infield mix during renovation.

Contains naturally occurring crystalline silica. Avoid creating and breathing dust during application. A safety data sheet is available on request. Values shown are typical and may vary slightly between lots.

California Proposition 65 warning: this product can expose you to crystalline silica, which is known to the State of California to cause cancer. For more information go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.
