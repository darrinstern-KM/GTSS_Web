GameTime White Strip™ Field Marking Powder

Features

  • Non-caustic. Made from ground calcium carbonate, not hydrated lime, which can burn skin, eyes and turf.
  • Lays a solid line. A fine, consistent grind flows through standard dry line markers without clumping or clogging.
  • Bright white. High-contrast lines that read clearly against dirt and grass.
  • One powder, every field. Baseball, softball, football, soccer and any other athletic field.
  • Economical. Sold by the pallet or the truckload, with single pallet orders available.
Recommended for: Baseball, softball, football, soccer, parks and recreation, youth leagues.
Application: Apply with a dry line marker. We supply 35 lb, 50 lb and 100 lb all-steel models.

As with any mineral powder, avoid creating and breathing dust during application. A safety data sheet is available on request. Values shown are typical and may vary slightly between lots.

California Proposition 65 warning: this product can expose you to crystalline silica, which is known to the State of California to cause cancer. For more information go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.
