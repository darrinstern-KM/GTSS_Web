Ordering

Credit terms

Net 30 on approved credit, purchase orders welcome, and all major cards accepted.

How you can pay

  • Net 30 terms on approved credit.
  • Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover.
  • Prepaid orders and checks by phone.

Purchase orders

We require a written purchase order, or a valid purchase order number subject to verification.

Opening a credit account

New customers requesting credit terms complete a credit application, which is subject to review and approval before the first order is released. To request an application, email info@gametimesportssystems.com or call 800.520.0512.

Prepayment

We may require prepayment for custom orders. Orders from outside the United States require prepayment before shipment.

Past due balances

Customers agree to pay invoices within the agreed terms. Past due balances are charged interest at 18% per year, or the highest rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower. Customers also agree to pay any fees, including attorney fees, incurred in collecting past due invoices.
