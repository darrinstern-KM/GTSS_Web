Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we have done

  • Text and controls meet color contrast requirements.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a link to skip to the main content.
  • Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers can navigate them.
  • Form fields have visible labels, required fields are marked in text, and errors are announced.
  • Animations respect your device's reduced motion setting, and the homepage animation can be paused.
  • Layouts adapt to phones and to text enlarged up to 200%.
  • Each page is tested with automated accessibility tools on desktop and phone sizes before it is published.

Known limitations

  • Some guides embed a video produced by a third party. We cannot guarantee the accuracy of its captions.
  • Our PDF spec sheets are designed for print. The same information is available as text on each product page.

Tell us if something does not work

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or need information in another format, email info@gametimesportssystems.com or call 800.520.0512. Tell us the page and the problem, and we will help you and fix it.
