Our story

It ain't just dirt

A family-owned company that has supplied the materials under America's ballfields since 2008.

Who we are

GameTime Sports Systems provides specialized groundskeeping products used by schools, municipalities, youth leagues and ballclubs. Our clay products are on baseball and softball fields across the country.

We are based in Northbrook, Illinois, and ship coast to coast, direct or through a network of distributors in 23 states. Our management team brings more than 40 years of experience serving businesses and government organizations.

What we believe

Our mission is to bring the highest quality products to every level of play. A professional club wants a field that looks the part. A parks department wants easy application and season-long performance. A school or youth league wants a consistent, safe surface. The right material does all three.
