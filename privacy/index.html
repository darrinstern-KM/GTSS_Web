Privacy policy

The short version: we collect only what you send us, we use it to answer you, and we never sell it.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by GameTime Sports Systems, LLC, 3015 White Pine Drive, Northbrook, IL 60062.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you request a quote we collect your name, email address, organization, type of organization, delivery ZIP code, your message and, if you provide it, your phone number.
  • Basic server logs. Like every website, our hosting provider records technical details of each request, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and troubleshooting.

That is all. We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising trackers, tracking pixels or analytics cookies.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not build profiles of visitors.

Because we do not sell or share personal information, there is nothing for you to opt out of. We honor Global Privacy Control signals in the same way.

How we use your information

  • To answer your request and prepare a quote.
  • To refer you to a GameTime distributor in your area, when one can serve you better. We share only the details needed to fulfill your request.
  • To send you field care tips by email, only if you ticked the box asking for them. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep ordinary business records.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process information on our behalf and only under our instructions: a customer relationship system that stores quote requests, an email service for subscribers who opted in, and our website host.

Video

Some guides include a video hosted by YouTube. The video does not load, and no information is sent to YouTube, unless you choose to play it. If you do, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and customer records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our business and legal record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email info@gametimesportssystems.com or call 800.520.0512. We will respond to every request, wherever you live. We will not treat you differently for making one.

Children

This website is for businesses and organizations. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

This website is served over an encrypted connection, and we limit access to the information you send us to the people who need it to respond.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@gametimesportssystems.com or 800.520.0512.
