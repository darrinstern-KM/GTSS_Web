Common questions

Straight answers

How much infield conditioner do I need?

Plan on 2.25 tons per 1,000 square feet at a depth of 1 inch. A full renovation takes about 8 tons (320 bags) for a field with 90 foot bases and about 6 tons (240 bags) for a field with 60 foot bases.

What is the difference between infield mix and infield conditioner?

Infield mix is the base soil of the skinned area. Infield conditioner is a calcined clay topdressing worked into the top layer to control moisture, reduce compaction and improve footing.

How do GameTime field materials ship?

In 50 lb bags (40 bags to a pallet, 22 pallets to a truckload), in 2,000 lb super sacks, or in bulk loads of up to 24 tons. Orders ship nationwide from Tennessee or through a GameTime distributor.

How do I dry a wet infield quickly?

Spread a calcined clay drying agent such as GameTime H2O/Abzorb™ on puddles and muddy spots. It absorbs standing water, will not stick to cleats and can be worked into the infield afterward.

Do you accept purchase orders?

Yes. We accept purchase orders from schools, parks departments and municipalities, offer net 30 terms on approved credit and take all major credit cards.
