Terms and conditions

How quotes, orders, shipping and returns work.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Quotes and orders

This website does not take orders or payments. Prices are provided by written quote. An order is accepted when we confirm it, and the terms on your quote and order confirmation apply to that order.

Pricing and specifications

We make every effort to honor quoted prices. We reserve the right to change prices due to increased costs and to correct errors in pricing or specifications. If a correct price is higher than the price stated, we will contact you for instructions before shipping.

Product specifications on this website and on our spec sheets are typical values. Actual results for a specific lot may vary slightly without affecting product performance.

Payment

Payment terms are net 30 with credit approval. We also accept major credit cards and company checks. We do not ship C.O.D. See our credit terms.

Sales tax

Sales tax is added where required. To qualify for exemption you must provide a current sales tax exemption or resale certificate. New customers should send exemption forms with the credit application or first order.

Shipping

Freight terms are stated on your quote. Arrival time depends on the method of shipment and the distance from the shipping point. If you need delivery by a specific date, tell us when you place the order.

Freight damage and shortages

If a shipment arrives short or damaged, note the shortage or damage on the freight delivery document before signing, and notify us immediately. We will gladly assist with your freight claim.

Returns

  • All returns must be authorized by us in advance. Call 800.520.0512 for a return authorization number, and include it with all correspondence and returns.
  • Returns must be sent within 30 days of the date the authorization is issued.
  • Items must be returned in their original condition and packaging.
  • Shipping charges are not refunded unless the merchandise is defective or was shipped incorrectly. Returned items are subject to return shipping charges and may be subject to a restocking fee of up to 25%.
  • Custom orders may not be returned unless the merchandise is defective or we made an error in filling the order.

We are not responsible for misuse, customer installation or improper storage.

Substitutions

On occasion we may ship a product that differs slightly from the one described. We will always substitute a product of equal or better quality and value. If your requirements prohibit substitution, tell us when you place the order.

Events beyond our control

We are not liable for failure to perform caused by acts of God or the elements, acts or delays of government authorities, riot, terrorism, sabotage or war, disruption of utilities, or other matters beyond our reasonable control.

Using this website

The guides, calculators and coverage charts on this website are provided for general information. Quantities are estimates. Confirm quantities and suitability for your field before ordering.
